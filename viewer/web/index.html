About this model

Positions come from the Tarmo5 CAD assembly plus our parts, as checked for clashes in the repo (commit 9d54384). The motor, H-bridge, ESP32, step-downs, battery board and servo are simplified blocks at their real sizes. Shocks aren't modelled. The animation is approximate: pivots were measured from the CAD holes, the suspension arms move as parallelograms, the tie rods slide instead of tilting, and the gearing shown is the 5:1 first stage only. Nothing was clash-checked while moving.
