# esp-rc v1: structure

v1 is the rolling chassis with our drivetrain, electronics and battery in place.
The body is a separate, swappable skin and is not part of v1
(`python -I assembly/build_assembly.py --body` adds the Tarmo5 pickup shell).

## View it
- Whole car: [`viewer/esp_rc_v1_view.stl`](../viewer/esp_rc_v1_view.stl) (lighter copy, within about 1 mm of the real parts)
- Only our parts, in place: [`viewer/esp_rc_v1_ours_view.stl`](../viewer/esp_rc_v1_ours_view.stl)

## What is in it
| Group | Parts |
|---|---|
| Tarmo5, reused unchanged | chassis (B01-B06, B10-B13), suspension, steering, gearbox, main gear, driveshafts, diff, bumper, wheels |
| Ours, printed | 540 motor standoff + plate, long pinion (3.3 mm bore), electronics tray v1, 2S battery module |
| Bought | 540 motor, IBT_2 H-bridge, ESP32 DevKit, MP1584 (ESP32), XL4015 (servo), 2S 40 A BMS, 2x 18650, MG946R servo |
| Removed | B09 motor mount, C08 pinion, D3542 motor, stock servo |

## Checks (assembly/build_assembly.py writes viewer/report.json)
Every pair of parts is intersected (exact boolean, or surface sampling where a mesh is not closed).
The contacts left are all intended:
- long pinion vs main gear C07: 2.1 mm3, the tooth mesh (the stock pinion shows the same)
- MG946R ears vs B04: 6.9 mm3, the ears sitting in the servo slot (the stock servo shows 7.5 mm3 in the same place)
- MG946R output spline vs horn: 14.8 mm3, the spline inside the horn

## Layout
- **Battery:** one 2S module (2x 18650) at the back of the B03 bay, BMS on the bay floor in front of it.
  The bay is 139.5 mm usable, so a second module does not fit; 4 cells would need another holder.
- **Electronics tray:** on top of B03, held by four M4x16 button heads through the 5 mm holes in the B03 top,
  with washers and nyloc nuts inside the bay (no heat-set inserts). Rear to front: XL4015, ESP32 + MP1584, H-bridge (heatsink down over vents).
  The highest point (H-bridge capacitor) is z = 62 mm in car coordinates.
- **Motor:** 540 on our standoff where the D3542 was; 1.6 mm from the upper rear arm at rest.

## Still open
- Step-down pockets use typical module sizes (MP1584 22x17, XL4015 51x26); check them against yours.
- Arm travel near the motor was checked only at rest.
- Nothing is printed except the bore strip and the pinion; no full test-fit yet.
